Who We Are

Sons, Brothers, Husbands, Fathers, Grandfathers

✠

The School of Saint Joseph is a Catholic men’s brotherhood formed to grow in knowing, loving, and serving our LORD. We are men who have heard the call to become who we were meant to be — and who refuse to walk that road alone.

We gather monthly for formation: food, fellowship, a talk, Lectio Divina, and prayer. We serve our parish, our priests, and our community. We hold each other accountable and remind each other that our identity is in CHRIST JESUS.

“To be encouraged by our brothers and to be an encouragement for our brothers. To remind and be reminded that our identity is in CHRIST JESUS.”
